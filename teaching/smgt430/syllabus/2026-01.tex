\textbf{\textsc{Topics}}
\begin{adjustwidth}{1cm}{0cm}
  The course is divided into $3$ units, each approximately $4$ weeks:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Unit $1$: Measuring Team Strength\\
    Pythagorean Formula, Bradley-Terry Model, ELO Ratings
    \item Unit $2$: Evaluating Player Talent\\
    Regression to the Mean, Hierarchical Models, Projections, Regularized Adjusted Plus-Minus
    \item Unit $3$: Valuing Event Outcomes\\
    Win Probability, Markov Chains, Markov Reward Processes, Markov Decision Processes
  \end{itemize}
\end{adjustwidth}


~\\
\textbf{\textsc{Course Objectives and Learning Outcomes}}
\begin{adjustwidth}{1cm}{0cm}
  After successfully completing this course, you will be able to:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Use R to apply theoretical statistical models to real-world sports data, and interpret the results.
    \item Calculate estimates of team and player strengths, adjusting for sample size and strength of schedule.
    \item Estimate in-game win probability models based on game state (e.g. score, possession, time remaining).
    \item Identify an interesting research problem in sports, solve it with data, and present your solution.
  \end{itemize}
\end{adjustwidth}

\textbf{\textsc{Policies}}
\begin{adjustwidth}{1cm}{0cm}
  \textbf{Attendance Policy.} If you notify me via email at least one hour before class that you will be absent, then I will excuse you from completing the exit ticket. Please do not expect a response to your email. It is your responsibility to check the schedule and educate yourself on the material you missed.

  \textbf{Generative AI Policy.} Generative AI may hinder your learning when used irresponsibly. To that end:
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item You may use generative AI to help you solve coding problems, but it is a violation of the honor code to submit AI-generated code that you do not understand or cannot explain.
    \item It is a violation of the honor code to submit AI-generated writing as your own work.
  \end{enumerate}

  \textbf{Late Policy.} Late submissions are subject to $10\%$ deduction per day.
